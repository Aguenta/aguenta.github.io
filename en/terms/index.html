Terms of use

Updated 16 November 2026

What Aguenta is

You choose the apps to block, for how long and how much you put on the line (from €5 to €50). If you hold out to the end, you pay nothing. If you give up before the end, you lose the amount. You must be at least 18 years old.

The money rule

When you start a challenge, the amount is held on your card, like a hotel deposit. If you hold out, the hold is released. If you give up, the amount is charged. If your card does not accept the hold, the challenge does not start.

The first 2 minutes

In the first 2 minutes of each challenge you can cancel without paying. A cancelled challenge does not count for anything.

Giving up

Giving up always asks for confirmation, with the amount shown. If you confirm, you lose the amount: there are no refunds for giving up by mistake. Deleting Aguenta, removing the Screen Time permission or deleting your account during a challenge also counts as giving up.

Who decides

Our server, not your phone's clock, keeps the time and decides whether you held out or gave up. If the time had already run out when you gave up, it counts as holding out.

Your account

The account is yours and yours alone. You can delete it at any time in Settings.
