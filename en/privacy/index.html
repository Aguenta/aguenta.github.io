Privacy policy

Updated 16 November 2026

Who we are

Aguenta is an app for putting your phone down with money on the line. For any question about your data, write to aguenta.app@gmail.com.

What data we keep

Your email, so you can sign in with a code. Your challenges: the apps you chose, the duration, the amount, when they started and how they ended. A reference to your Stripe customer. The date you confirmed you are 18 or older. The language of your phone, so our emails reach you in that language. We do not keep your card numbers.

What we use it for

To let you sign in, time each challenge, hold, charge or release the amount, and send you emails about your account (the sign-in code, the welcome email and the receipt for a charge). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Supabase: stores your account and challenges, on servers in Ireland (European Union). Stripe: stores your card and makes the holds and charges. Sentry: receives the app's errors, so we can fix them, on servers in the European Union. PostHog: receives the steps you take in the app (for example, "started a challenge", with the amount and the duration), on servers in the European Union. Sentry and PostHog receive a number that identifies you and technical data about your device (for example, the model and system version), never your email, your card or the apps you chose.

How long

For as long as you have an account. When you delete it, we delete the account and the challenges. The records of charges that the law requires us to keep stay with Stripe for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it or delete it. To delete everything, use Delete account in Settings. For anything else, write to aguenta.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, CNPD) or to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live.
