Privacy policy

Updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

Aguenta is an app for putting your phone down with money on the line. For any question about your data, write to aguenta.app@gmail.com.

What data we keep

Your email, so you can sign in with a code. Your challenges: the apps you chose, the duration, the amount, when they started and how they ended. A reference to your Stripe customer. The date you confirmed you are 18 or older (we ask for your date of birth only to work out your age, on your phone: we do not store it). The date you accepted the Terms and which version you accepted. The language of your phone, so our emails reach you in that language. We do not keep your card numbers.

What we use it for

To let you sign in, time each challenge, hold, charge or release the amount, and send you emails about your account (the sign-in code, the welcome email and the receipt for a charge). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Supabase: stores your account and challenges, on servers in Ireland (European Union). Stripe: stores your card and makes the holds and charges. Google: sends our emails (the sign-in code, the welcome email and the receipts), through Gmail. Sentry: receives the app's errors, so we can fix them, on servers in the European Union. PostHog: only if you accept the usage statistics, receives the steps you take in the app (for example, "started a challenge", with the amount and the duration), on servers in the European Union. Sentry and PostHog receive a number that identifies you and technical data about your device (for example, the model and system version), never your email, your card or the apps you chose.

Legal basis

Contract: your account, the challenges, the payments and the service emails (the code, the welcome email and the receipts) are needed to provide the service. Legal obligation: we keep the records of charges that the law requires. Legitimate interest: error reports (Sentry), so the app works well and securely. Consent: usage statistics (PostHog) are only switched on if you accept; you can change your mind at any time in Settings › Usage statistics.

Transfers outside the European Union

Stripe and Google are United States companies and may process data outside the European Union. They do so under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Supabase, Sentry and PostHog store the data on servers in the European Union.

How long

For as long as you have an account. When you delete it, we delete the account and the challenges. The records of charges that the law requires us to keep stay with Stripe for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it or delete it. To get a copy, use Export my data in Settings. To delete everything, use Delete account in Settings. For anything else, write to aguenta.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, CNPD) or to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live.
