Support

How do I cancel a challenge without paying?

In the first 2 minutes of each challenge, open the challenge and tap "Cancel without paying". The held amount is released and you pay nothing.

What if I give up after the 2 minutes?

Giving up always asks for confirmation, with the amount shown. If you confirm, the amount is charged to your card and you get a receipt by email.

Are there refunds?

There are no refunds for giving up by mistake: that is the rule. If you think something went wrong (for example, you were charged after the time ran out), write to us and we will look into it with you.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account › Delete account. We delete the account and the challenges. A running challenge counts as giving up (in the first 2 minutes it is cancelled without paying).

How do I change my card?

In the app: Account › Change card. Your card details are stored by Stripe, never by Aguenta.

The app is not blocking the apps

Check that you gave Aguenta the Screen Time permission in Settings › Screen Time. Removing the permission during a challenge counts as giving up.

For any other question or complaint, write to aguenta.app@gmail.com. We reply by email, usually on the same day.
